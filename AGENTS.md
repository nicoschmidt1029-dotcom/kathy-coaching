## Website languages

- Every piece of visitor-facing content added or changed in English must also
  be implemented in German and Slovak in the same change.
- Do not leave English fallback copy visible on the German or Slovak website.
- Keep program facts, prices, buttons, legal information, metadata and form
  feedback consistent across all three locales.
